Good to know

Questions parents and dancers ask

What ages can dance at Flow?

Our Imagine Preschool Program is for 2–4 year olds. Our Recreational Dance Program runs from 5 years to adult.

Which dance styles can my child learn?

You can take classes in Ballet, Jazz, Contemporary, Hip Hop, Acro and Musical Theatre.

Can we try a class before enrolling?

Yes. Email info@flowdanceacademy.nz or call 027 540 6226 to book a FREE trial class.

Can dancers sit exams?

Yes. Recreational dancers have the opportunity to sit annual examinations with ATOD.

Where is the dance studio in Christchurch?

You'll find us at 10 Eglinton St, Avondale, Christchurch. Open in Google Maps.

Do you hire out costumes?

Yes. We have a wide range of costumes and props available for you to hire. Please contact us for more information.
